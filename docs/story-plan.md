# Story plan: NC State campus voting

## Scope

Keep the main comparison simple: **2022 general election vs. 2026 general election**.

Do not use 2024 as a primary comparison. The 2024 presidential election can be revisited later only if there is a specific reporting reason.

## Question

What changed in voting behavior around NC State after the campus early-voting location moved away from Talley Student Union?

## Early voting

Compare 2022 Talley with the 2026 NC State-area early-voting site using:

- total ballots cast at the site
- site share of all Wake County early voting
- ballots by day
- age
- party registration
- race
- ethnicity
- gender / sex field as provided by the source
- same-day registration
- precincts of voters who used the site

Important: early voters may use any early-voting site in their county, so a voter at the NC State site is not necessarily an NC State student.

## Election Day

Compare 2022 and 2026 using:

- Wake County Election Day voters by precinct
- voting-method totals
- age groups
- party registration
- race
- ethnicity
- sex field as provided by NCSBE
- official Election Day polling-place assignments

Important: Election Day voters are assigned polling places by residential address. Do not treat an NC State-area precinct as equivalent to "students." If campus-area precincts are selected for the story, document the exact precincts and why they were included.

## November workflow

1. Preserve the 2022 baseline as the locked comparison dataset.
2. After 2026 early voting ends, download the final/most complete NCSBE early-voting file and build the same measures.
3. After Election Day, add the 2026 polling-place information and preliminary totals.
4. Wait for finalized 2026 voter-history statistics before making final precinct/demographic Election Day comparisons.
5. Validate totals against published NCSBE/Wake County totals before publication.

## Possible graphics

- 2022 Talley vs. 2026 NC State-area site turnout
- age distribution at each early-voting site
- early-voting site share of Wake County turnout
- daily early-voting curves
- party/race/gender composition comparisons
- map of 2022 vs. 2026 voting locations
- Election Day turnout by selected campus-area precincts, clearly labeled as precinct residents rather than students
