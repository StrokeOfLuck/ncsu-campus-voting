Election Day

PRIMARY DATA

NCSBE — 2022 Historical Voter History Statistics

Aggregate source for Election Day participation by county, precinct, voting method and demographics.

NCSBE documents fields including county_desc, precinct_abbrv, age range, party, race, ethnicity, sex, total_voters and voting_method. The historical stats file does not contain individual voter rows.
