PRIMARY DATA

NCSBE — 2022 Nov. 8 Absentee / Early Voting by County

Main voter-level source for the 2022 early-voting baseline.

NCSBE's guidance says to identify early voters by filtering abs_req_type to EARLY VOTING and ballot_rtn_status to ACCEPTED. The file also contains fields used for demographic and site analysis.

2022 TALLEY BASELINE

Wake County — Early Voting Sites by Election & Ballots Cast

Wake County reports 10,390 ballots cast at NCSU Talley Student Union during 2022 general-election early voting.

Use this as a validation total for the site-level filtering of the NCSBE file.

REPORTING NOTE

Privacy / publication

The official election files can contain voter-level personal information. Keep the raw archive local and publish aggregate counts and percentages in this repository rather than names, addresses, NCIDs or other voter-level identifiers.
